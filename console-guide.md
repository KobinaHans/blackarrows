# TEKKO Console guide

The console is where TEKKO staff manage the website and respond to enquiries. Sign in at your console address, for example `https://console.tekkoengineering.com`.

## Signing in

1. Your administrator invites you by email. Open the link and choose a password: at least 12 characters, with upper-case and lower-case letters and a number.
2. Sign in with your work email and password, or with **Continue with Microsoft** if single sign-on is enabled.
3. Open **Account and security** and set up two-factor authentication with an authenticator app. Store your backup codes somewhere safe.

## Roles

| Role | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Everything, including managing other owners |
| Administrator | Everything except managing owners |
| Content editor | Edit and publish services, experience, industries, pages and media |
| Business development | View, update, assign and export enquiries |
| Viewer | Read-only access to content, enquiries and settings |

If a page says you don't have access, ask an administrator to change your role.

## Enquiries

- **Enquiries** lists every request from the website contact form. **Open** shows those still in progress.
- Open an enquiry to see the message and contact details, and to email the person directly.
- Move the enquiry through the pipeline: **New → Contacted → Qualified → Proposal sent → Won or Lost**. Mark junk as **Spam**.
- Set a **priority** and **assign** the enquiry to a colleague.
- Add **internal notes** after calls or site visits. Notes are never visible to the enquirer.
- **Export CSV** downloads the current view, for example for monthly reporting.

## Website content

### Services, Experience and Industries

- Use **New** to add an entry. New entries start as **Draft** and are not shown on the website.
- **Publish** makes an entry visible. **Unpublish** hides it again. **Archive** hides it and keeps it for reference.
- **Preview on website** opens the entry on the real website before it is published, so you can see exactly how it will look. A striped bar across the top reminds you that you are in preview; **Exit preview** returns to the published site. Preview shows what is saved, so save first. The link is personal and expires after two hours: open a new one from the console rather than forwarding it.
- **Order** controls position: lower numbers appear first.
- Add an **image** from the media library. Without one, the website shows a technical drawing.
- On services, fill in **Questions and answers**. They appear on the page and help search engines.
- The **History** panel shows who changed what and when.

### Pages

Edit the home page (headline, facts, lifecycle stages, client value, closing message), the about page, and the privacy policy and terms. Legal pages use simple formatting: `## Heading`, `- bullet`, `**bold**`, `[link text](https://…)`. Use **Preview** before saving.

### Media library

- Drag photographs or PDFs onto the upload area (JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, GIF or PDF, up to 10 MB each).
- Click a file to add **alt text**: a short description of the image for screen readers and search engines.
- **Used on** shows which pages use a file before you delete it.

### Site settings

- **Company and network:** name, contact details, address, business hours, LinkedIn, client types, leadership credentials, and the countries shown on the network map.
- **Search:** default page titles and description, and whether search engines may index the site.
- **Announcement:** a notice across the top of every page, such as an office closure or event.
- **Notifications:** who receives new-enquiry emails, and the automatic reply sent to enquirers.

## Good practice

- Only publish statements, figures and credentials you can substantiate.
- Get written permission before publishing client names or project photographs.
- Review the audit log monthly (administrators).
- Remove access for leavers on their last day: **Team → Disable access**.
